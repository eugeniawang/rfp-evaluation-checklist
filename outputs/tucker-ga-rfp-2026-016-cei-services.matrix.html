What this is

This page shows how City of Tucker · Construction, Engineering and Inspection (CEI) Services will be scored. It's built from tucker-ga-rfp-2026-016-cei-services.pdf, the actual RFP document, not a summary of it. The RFP says proposals are scored out of 100 points, and the items below add up to 100. Below that, you'll find 3 things that earn points, 17 rules that can get a proposal thrown out before scoring even starts, and 5 other things the RFP says about scoring that aren't tied to a specific scored item.
