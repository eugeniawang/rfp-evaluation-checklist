What this is

This page shows how @@TITLE@@ will be scored. It's built from @@PDF@@, the actual RFP document, not a summary of it. @@TOTAL_SENTENCE@@ Below that, you'll find @@ROW_COUNT@@ things that earn points, @@DISQ_COUNT@@ rules that can get a proposal thrown out before scoring even starts, and @@CNM_COUNT@@ lines from the RFP we found but couldn't tie to a specific scored item.

How to read this page

  • This page has three parts: How you'll be scored (the points table), What gets you thrown out (rules that can disqualify a proposal no matter how good it is), and Things we found but couldn't place (RFP sentences we spotted but couldn't tie to a specific scored item).
  • A grey box like Page 5, line 36 is a button. Click it to see the exact words in the RFP, highlighted, so you can check our work against the real document.
  • "The RFP doesn't say" means exactly that. The RFP is silent on that point. We never guess or fill it in for you.
  • The blank, striped boxes are for your team. We don't know who at your firm owns what, so those are left empty on purpose for someone to fill in.
  • Everything on this page is a fact from the RFP, not our opinion. We don't judge your bid, rank your chances, or suggest a strategy. We only report what the RFP itself says.
